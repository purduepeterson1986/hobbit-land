Hobbit Land

Roll the die and carry the Ring from the Shire to Mount Doom. Land on a card space and turn over a card. Land exactly on a fork and you may find a shortcut.

Players

Each player chooses a hobbit. Set any player to Computer to have the game play that hobbit.

How to play

  1. On your turn, press Roll. Your hobbit walks that many spaces along the road.
  2. If you stop on a card space, a card is turned over. It may send you forward, send you back, or keep you where you are. Press Continue and your hobbit moves. That move never turns over another card.
  3. If you stop exactly on a fork, your next move takes the shortcut. Pass a fork without stopping and you stay on the long road.
  4. Stop on a resting place (Bree, Rivendell or Lothlórien) and you get +1 on your next roll.
  5. About one card in fifteen is a rare card: the Eagles carry you 5 spaces, you swap places with another hobbit, or Gollum steals your next turn.
  6. Reach Mount Doom first to win. Play goes on until every place is decided.

The card spaces and the scenery change every game.
